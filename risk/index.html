Read this first

Trading gold (XAUUSD) with leverage can lose money quickly. TBWN Signal publishes structured trade information. It is not advice to buy or sell, and every trade you place is your own decision.

Leverage and rapid loss

  • Leverage magnifies gains and losses. You can lose your whole deposit, and with some brokers more than your deposit.
  • Gold can move hundreds of pips within minutes, especially around economic releases and market opens.
  • A stop loss limits a planned loss but does not guarantee it: prices can gap past it.

Your execution will differ from ours

  • Spreads, commissions, slippage, requotes and delays differ between brokers and between moments in time.
  • The price on your platform may not match the price used in a TBWN record.
  • A setup can reach its entry on one platform and not on another.

No guarantee of results

No setup is certain, and past results, including every figure on this website, are not a promise of future ones. Many recorded results are managed closes or break-evens, and a stop loss is reached in some cases. See how the record is counted.

Your responsibility

  • Decide the position size from your own balance and risk limit. The planner is a calculator, not a recommendation.
  • Never trade money you cannot afford to lose, and never trade only to qualify for access to anything.
  • Seek independent financial advice if you are unsure whether trading suits you.

What TBWN is not

TBWN does not manage money, hold customer trading funds, place trades on your behalf, or see your trading account.

TBWN does not accept deposits or manage funds. It provides structured trade plans and signals only; every trading decision and every order is yours.
